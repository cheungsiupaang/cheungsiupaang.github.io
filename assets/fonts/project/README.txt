Project-page fonts

Google Sans and Noto Sans are served locally as unmodified Latin-subset variable
WOFF2 fonts. Their SIL Open Font License notices are included in this directory.

Reference: https://nkrobotlab.github.io/LAMPS/
Source: https://github.com/nkrobotlab/LAMPS
The reference uses Google Sans for the title and authors, and Noto Sans for the
body and resource links. Its title is 2.55rem, weight 600, with line-height 1.125
and normal letter spacing. Author names are 1.25rem, weight 400.
